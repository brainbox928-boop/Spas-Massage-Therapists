Quick Neck & Shoulder Self-Massage Routine

Sit comfortably in a chair. This 5-minute routine relieves tension built up from desk work, driving, or stress — no tools required.

1
Warm the Muscles

Rub your palms together briskly for 10 seconds, then cup them over the back of your neck for a few breaths to bring warmth to the area.

2
Trapezius Kneading

Reach across your body and squeeze the opposite shoulder's trapezius muscle between your fingers and palm. Knead gently for 30 seconds per side.

3
Base-of-Skull Circles

Using your fingertips, press into the base of your skull and make small, slow circles for 30 seconds to release tension headaches at the source.

4
Gentle Neck Rotations

Slowly tilt your head toward each shoulder, holding for 15 seconds per side. Breathe deeply and let gravity deepen the stretch — never force it.

5
Seal With Stillness

Rest your hands in your lap, close your eyes, and take 3 slow, deep breaths to let the relaxation settle in before returning to your day.

Tip: Practice this routine daily for best results, and always stop if you feel sharp pain.
